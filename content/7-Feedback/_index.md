> Here, you can freely share your personal opinions about your experience participating in the First Cloud Journey program. This will help the FCJ team improve any shortcomings based on the following aspects:

### Overall Evaluation

1. Working Environment
   The working environment is exceptionally friendly and open. I greatly appreciate that FCJ members are consistently willing to help, even outside of working hours. The workspace itself is tidy and comfortable, which aids focus.

Suggestion: While the atmosphere is excellent, incorporating more social gatherings or team bonding activities would further strengthen relationships and team cohesion.

2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin
   The support has been outstanding. My mentor provides detailed guidance and clear explanations, consistently encouraging me to ask questions. I especially value the approach of being allowed to tackle and solve problems independently first. The admin team effectively supports all necessary administrative and logistical tasks.

3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major
   The assigned tasks demonstrated high relevance to my academic major, allowing me to reinforce my foundational knowledge. Crucially, the program also introduced me to new, practical areas I had not encountered in university, providing valuable skills acquisition.

4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities
   I have gained significant new skills, including the use of project management tools, effective teamwork, and professional corporate communication. The mentor's shared real-world experience has been invaluable in helping me better plan my career path.

5. Company Culture & Team Spirit
   The company culture is highly positive and respectful. Team spirit is strong, especially during urgent projects, where everyone regardless of position cooperates and supports each other. This environment made me feel like a valued, real part of the team.

6. Internship Policies / Benefits
   The internship allowance and flexible working hours are appreciated. The opportunity to attend internal training sessions is a significant and valuable benefit.
